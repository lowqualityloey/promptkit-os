## 1. [P2] Require the family identifier when reporting reuse

Source: [recipe lines 103-105](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/2c608e4e21d1c431c0449d32a36ed31844d8b24e/docs/recipes/auth-session.md#L103-L105), [result contract lines 82-85](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/2c608e4e21d1c431c0449d32a36ed31844d8b24e/docs/recipes/auth-session.md#L82-L85).

The introduced RotationResult interface permits `{ status: "reuse_detected" }` without a familyId. If a losing conditional exchange returns that valid result after the winning exchange creates a successor, the wrapper skips revokeFamily and reports that the family was revoked. The successor remains usable. This violates issue #465's family-revocation requirement without requiring a database failure.

Use a discriminated union requiring familyId for reuse_detected and revoke unconditionally in that branch. Define success with a required successor identifier as well.

## 2. [P2] Verify the published recipe's revocation behavior

Source: [test store lines 72-75](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/2c608e4e21d1c431c0449d32a36ed31844d8b24e/scripts/tests/verify-auth-session-concurrency.mjs#L72-L75), [copied wrapper lines 17-40](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/2c608e4e21d1c431c0449d32a36ed31844d8b24e/scripts/tests/verify-auth-session-concurrency.mjs#L17-L40).

The script copies the recipe's function and adds a family_revoked branch absent from its published status contract and implementation. Its fake store also revokes the family before returning reuse_detected. Therefore, removing the wrapper's revokeFamily call would still leave the post-revocation assertions passing. Changes to the Markdown recipe do not affect the independent copied function at all. The harness does not protect the documented implementation against the revocation failure in finding 1.

Exercise the actual published recipe, or enforce automatic parity with it. Align the revoked-family result contract and add a scenario whose adapter reports reuse without revoking internally so that the wrapper's mandatory revocation is observed.
