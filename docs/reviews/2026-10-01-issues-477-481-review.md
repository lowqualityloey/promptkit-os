## #477 — Amend the proposed fixes

**1. Replace the literal array-slice prescription.** When the block begins at index zero, `$lines[0..($startIndex - 1)]` becomes `$lines[0..-1]`; PowerShell produces a range rather than an empty prefix. A block ending at the last line similarly needs an explicit empty-suffix case. Splitting/rejoining can also change surrounding line endings or trailing-newline bytes.

Require replacement from validated boundaries in the original content, with empty-prefix/suffix handling and byte preservation outside the managed block. [Current boundary validation and replacement](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/61e7e36584468cd8e95de8c29e358eea49a72d52/init.ps1#L706).

**2. Define actual added-line parsing.** The suggested zero-context diff flags still emit deleted lines and `+++` filename headers. Require hunk-aware parsing and quiet/redacted output; failed diff reads must not become a clean result. [Current leaking command](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/61e7e36584468cd8e95de8c29e358eea49a72d52/workflows/commit.md#L101).

**3. Make template exemptions exact.** Removing `test` alone leaves substring exclusions such as `.env.example.production` or `.env.sample-secret`. Check exact approved basenames instead of filtering entire status rows. [Current gate](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/61e7e36584468cd8e95de8c29e358eea49a72d52/workflows/commit.md#L94).

**Add acceptance cases:**

- Block at the beginning/end, inline marker examples, CRLF/LF, and unchanged surrounding bytes.
- Deleted credential-bearing probe produces no match or raw output; added probe is detected without raw output.
- Scan failure is reported as incomplete/failed.
- Exact public templates are permitted; `.env.test` and template-looking credential suffixes are flagged.

## #481 — Correct evidence and connect the adapters

**1. Correct the color claims.** The actual token is `oklch(52% 0.22 250 / 0.35)`, not the claimed RGBA equivalent. Use the conservative 2.44154:1 maximum for 35%-opaque color on white. The suggested replacements measure approximately **5.17:1** for `#2563eb` and **6.70:1** for `#1d4ed8`; remove the claim exceeding 7:1. An offset alone does not increase ring-versus-white contrast. [Actual token](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/61e7e36584468cd8e95de8c29e358eea49a72d52/templates/design-tokens-spec.md#L129).

[Current gate](https://github.com/lowqualityloey/promptkit-os/blob/61e7e36584468cd8e95de8c29e358eea49a72d52/docs/recipes/webhook-idempotency.md#L71), [Stripe verification](https://docs.stripe.com/webhooks/signature).
