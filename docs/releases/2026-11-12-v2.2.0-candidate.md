Corrective doctor, installer, courier, and quickstart changes are patch-level, and the release-record, benchmark-documentation, CI, and test-fixture work is maintenance. Under the documented precedence major > minor > patch, the greatest effective impact is minor. No removal, rename, or breaking contract replacement was found, so major is not justified.`
