## Coverage and evidence

Every public note maps to at least one effective commit in the evaluated range. N01 is the only additive change and drives the minor candidate; N02-N08 are corrective changes to shipped behavior or shipped documentation. The v2.2.0 release dossier, the `docs/BENCHMARKS.md` provenance-anchor restatements, the task-record CI evidence entries, and the test-only rubric, fixture, and contract controls are maintenance because they do not alter the installed workflow, protocol, template, installer, courier, or measurement contract consumed by PromptKit OS users.

The CHANGELOG [2.3.0] section carries seven entries: two Added for N01 and its review fixes, one Fixed for N02, and four Maintenance. Three of the four Maintenance entries carry N03-N07, which the CHANGELOG classifies as requiring no user action even though they correct shipped user-facing documentation; this record carries them as public notes because a reader of the README, FAQ, or shipped profile table observes the corrected text. The mapping is stated in the Derived Entries field above and is not a coverage gap.

No breaking contract change is present, so no migration entry is required. The boundary is an added instruction in both directives and an added protocol section; the matcher changes are confined to the offline grading harness and alter no shipped command; and the documentation corrections restate existing behavior rather than changing it.
