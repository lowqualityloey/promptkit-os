## Coverage and evidence

Every public note maps to at least one effective commit in the evaluated range. The v2.1.0 release-record maintenance, the ceremony Task Record and benchmark documentation, the CI wiring and test-fixture alignment, and the enforcement documentation are maintenance because they do not alter the installed workflow, protocol, template, installer, courier, or measurement contract consumed by PromptKit OS users. The CHANGELOG [2.2.0] section carries one entry per reviewed change.

No breaking contract change is present, so no migration entry is required. The ceremony-overhead scripts are new files that nothing else invokes, and the `check-setup-assert` recovery-command query mode is a new flag that leaves the existing invocation, output and exit codes unchanged.
