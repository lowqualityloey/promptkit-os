# Senior Developer Engineering Activities & Simulations

Welcome to the Senior Developer Activity Suite. These simulations are designed to challenge and hone your capabilities in real-world software engineering domains, including distributed system design, clean architectural refactoring, subtle concurrency debugging, and modern accessible design systems.

## How to Execute an Activity
1. Pick a simulation from the catalog.
2. Activate your AI assistant with the specified workflow (e.g., `pk:plan for 01-system-design-spike` or `pk:tutor architect`).
3. Work through the multi-stage milestones using the 3-tier Socratic guidance from your AI mentor.
4. When finished, run `pk:retro` to capture your architectural decisions in `./docs/adrs/`, update `.promptkit/notes/progress-journal.md`, and advance your competencies in `.promptkit/notes/skill-matrix.md`.
